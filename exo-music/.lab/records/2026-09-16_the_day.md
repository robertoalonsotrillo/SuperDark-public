# 16 September 2026 — the day the charter passed

A long session. This consolidates it, because several of the day's findings
correct earlier records and a reader should not have to reconstruct the order.

## What changed, in one line each

**The blow-up is fixed at its root.** `rq` is bandwidth divided by centre
frequency, so `rq.clip(0.5, 2.5)` bounded the ratio and bounded the bandwidth not
at all. Above **cutoff 8820 Hz** the bandwidth passes Nyquist and BPF diverges —
to 1.35e18 at 9680 Hz, 9.78e28 at 18 kHz. The boundary is Nyquist exactly: 8800
peaks at 0.0568, 8900 at 6.29e12. Fixed at both call sites by clipping bandwidth
as well as ratio.

**`m3_no_clipping` passed for the first time in the project's history**, and the
charter with it: **satisfied on 7 of 8 metrics** over 838 windows, m3 at 0.000.

**Shadow comparison 1 of 5 completed and gated clean** — the first comparison ever
to clear the gate, all ten hard invariants passing.

**A second factory exists**, deployable to an SSD, with its own learned state,
gated git updates, and an email watchdog.

## The hunt, and the six wrong answers

Each of these was proposed by me and killed by evidence that existed *before* the
proposal:

| explanation | refuted by |
|---|---|
| corrupt grains | all 48,162 grains in every pool cap at 0.9990 |
| reverb feedback | all four reverb buses are literally `[0]` |
| NaN / inf | the peaks are finite; `CheckBadValues` would have zeroed them |
| polyphony | BLOWUP windows average **1.11** macro voices against 2.01; one blew up at **macro = 0** |
| `\amp` out of range | bounded across all 177 written machines |
| the DSP stage | `dres`, `ddly`, `dblr`, `dall` all clipped by the caller |

**And BPF itself was cleared once, wrongly, by me.** An early sweep tested cutoff
at `rq = 0.5` — the *narrowest* setting, chosen on the belief that narrow meant
high-Q meant most dangerous. The hazard runs the other way: wide rq at high
cutoff. That sweep tested the safe corner thoroughly and stopped 820 Hz short.

**What actually found it** was a probe inside the voice reporting its own peak and
parameters. It caught the fault in **thirteen minutes**. The lesson is not subtle:
instrumenting the thing costs less than reasoning about the thing.

**`m3` was vindicated, not mis-priced.** I had proposed re-pricing it as an
over-sensitive tripwire. It was correctly detecting a broken transport for weeks.
The re-pricing is withdrawn.

## Claims retired today

- **The `dr` attribution.** "macro=dr on 186 of 186 blow-ups" was reported as a
  localisation and had **no denominator** — slot machines were logged only on
  failures. `dr` was not the diverging voice. Fixed: `SLOTS` on every window.
- **Polyphony accumulation** as the mechanism, refuted twice over — by an offline
  render peaking at 0.487 on the *current* code at the worst corner, and by the
  live `VOICES` data.
- **"One minute of trouble in four hours"** — the run was 49 minutes, not four
  hours. The correction makes the factory look worse and is in the record.

## The pattern, now at eight instances

A parameter or rule that exists, carries an intended value, and is never reached.
Today added four to the four already known (`self_ingest_weight` unread,
`PatternGrammar` unwired, the ingest daemon watching an empty directory,
`attention_weight` never once running):

5. **`one_change_at_a_time`** written in the policy and wired to nothing. A
   candidate differing in four things passed the gate without comment.
6. **`MUTABLE` accepted and dropped** by `start_loop.sh`, plus four hardcoded
   `$W/mutable/policy.json` reads — a replica would have learned into the
   primary's state.
7. **`--until`** taken by `build_arm_audio` since it was written and passed by
   nothing, so a champion's audio ran to *now* and swallowed the candidate's.
8. **`recurrence_sweep`** holding a veto it could never satisfy: unwired *on
   purpose* (three calibration arms, not a per-arm scorer), and NOT WIRED vetoes
   — so a measure structurally incapable of joining a paired comparison vetoed
   every one of them, permanently.

`FreezeVerb`'s dead `temp - LPF.ar(temp, lpf);` line belongs to the same family:
a parameter accepted, documented, and doing nothing.

**The rule this project now works by: grep for the consumer before experimenting
on a knob.** Eight instances is no longer a coincidence, it is a property of how
this system was built.

## The harness's first real use, and what it caught

Put through end to end on the Nyquist fix. It **refused**, and every reason was
about me rather than the factory:

- the **seal had been broken for four hours** — a scorer modified at 09:37 with
  the `adopt` never run, so every number produced in that window was
  unattributable and nothing said so
- **four of sixteen contamination faults were my own restarts** that afternoon,
  each one recorded
- `min_windows` failed on arithmetic I should have done first

Then, on the attention comparison, three defects that blocked **every** shadow
comparison rather than that one: the filename-based pairing rule, the
whole-journal contamination count, and `recurrence_sweep`'s impossible veto.

Roberto's decision on pairing — **disjoint scored ranges count as paired** — is
recorded in `eval_compare.py` with the reasoning, because it is a choice about
what counts as evidence and not a bug fix. I did not edit the gate to turn its
own BLOCKED into a pass.

That the factory is good. Target 5 — "worth an hour" — is judged by the ear, and
today was spent almost entirely on defects.

That escape from *c* has happened. Per the 15 Sept amendment, self-ingestion and
persistent memory both run through paths the architect wrote.

## The honest summary

The defensible claim from today is not that the factory works. It is that **the
instruments caught their author, repeatedly, and were believed over him**:
the charter's never-tolerated metric was vindicated against my own proposal to
re-price it; the rig refused its first real use over my unadopted seal; six of my
explanations were killed by evidence I then went and gathered; and a rule the
project believed it was enforcing turned out to be wired to nothing — for the
fifth, sixth, seventh and eighth time.
